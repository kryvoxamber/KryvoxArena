Rules

Registration karne ka matlab hai ki tumne ye saare rules maan liye hain.

Registration aur payment

  • Registration sirf successful payment ke baad valid hai.
  • Ek payment sirf ek squad ke liye chalega.
  • Slots limited hain, pehle aao pehle pao.
  • Form mein UID aur naam sahi likho. Galat detail pe slot cancel ho sakta hai.

Match se pehle

  • Room ID aur password match se 15 min pehle Telegram group mein milega.
  • Sirf registered players hi khelenge. Player badalna ho to match se pehle admin ko batao.
  • Late join pe refund ya re-entry nahi.
  • Room details kisi ko share mat karo.
  • Solo mein sirf registered player hi khelega. Account share ya kisi aur se khelwana allowed nahi.

Fair play aur ban

  • Hack, third-party tools ya emulator (jahan allowed nahi) pe permanent ban.
  • Teaming, glitch abuse aur stream sniping allowed nahi.
  • Gaali-galoch ya abusive behaviour pe disqualification.
  • Ban hone pe entry fee wapas nahi milegi.

Result aur payout

  • Match ke result ke screenshots apne paas rakho.
  • Koi dispute ho to match khatam hone ke 30 min ke andar screenshot ya video bhejo.
  • Winner ka faisla match ke final result se hoga. Scoring ki detail tournament poster pe di jayegi.
  • Admin ka decision final hoga.
  • Most Kills bonus: match mein sabse zyada kills karne wale team (Solo mein player) ko milega, screenshot proof ke saath. Kills barabar hone pe behtar placement wale ko bonus milega, placement bhi barabar ho to bonus barabar baant diya jayega. Bonus top 3 ke prize ke alawa hai.
  • Prize top 3 teams ko milega (1st, 2nd, 3rd). Mode ke hisaab se amount Match details mein dekho. Payout 24 ghante ke andar winners ke UPI pe, proof Instagram pe.
  • Hamari taraf se match cancel hone pe refund ya agle match mein shift. Slots poore na bharne par bhi match cancel ho sakta hai.

FAQ

Payment ke baad slot kab confirm hoga?

Payment verify hote hi slot confirm hota hai aur tumhe Telegram group mein add kar diya jata hai.

Payment kat gaya par slot confirm nahi hua?

Payment ka screenshot aur transaction ID Telegram pe bhej do. Hum check karenge.

Kya refund milega?

Sirf tab jab match hamari taraf se cancel ho. Us case mein refund ya agle match mein slot shift milega. Late join ya ban pe refund nahi hota.

Most Kills bonus kaise milta hai?

Jis team ke match mein sabse zyada kills hote hain use Mode ke hisaab se alag bonus milta hai (amount Match details mein dekho). Kills barabar hone pe behtar placement wale ko milta hai. Result screen ka screenshot sambhal ke rakho. Bonus ka payout bhi 24 ghante ke andar hota hai.

Room ID kab aur kahan milega?

Match se 15 minute pehle Telegram group mein. Sirf verified squads ko group mein add kiya jata hai.

Squad mein 4 se kam players ho to?

Squad ke liye pure 4 players chahiye. Kam players ho to Duo (2 players) ya Solo (akele) match mein register karo.

Solo match mein kya alag hai?

Solo mein har player akela khelta hai aur uska apna score banta hai. Entry fee aur slots mode ke hisaab se alag hote hain, Modes section dekho.

Kya koi bhi khel sakta hai?

Paid matches mein sirf 18 saal ya usse upar ke players khel sakte hain.

Prize kab milta hai?

Result confirm hone ke 24 ghante ke andar winners ke UPI pe. Payout ka screenshot Instagram page pe post hota hai.

UID kaise dekhein?

Free Fire mein game kholo aur apne profile/avatar pe tap karo, naam ke paas UID dikhega. BGMI mein profile pe tap karo, wahan Character ID dikhega. Game ke version ke hisaab se jagah thodi alag ho sakti hai. UID ko copy karke registration mein paste karo.

Custom room kaise join karein?

Game kholo, Custom Room ya Room wale option mein jao, Join pe tap karo, aur Telegram group mein mila Room ID aur password daalo. Room details match se thodi der pehle hi aati hain.

Result screenshot kaise lein?

Match khatam hone pe result ya ranking screen ka screenshot lo, jisme team ka naam, placement aur kills dikhein. Dispute ho to 30 minute ke andar admin ko Telegram pe bhejo.

Kisi pe cheating ka shak ho to?

Match khatam hone ke 30 minute ke andar screenshot ya video ke saath admin ko Telegram pe message karo. Check karke action liya jayega.

Terms, refund aur privacy

Terms of service

Kryvox Arena mein registration karke tum in terms ko maante ho. Tournament Rules section aur poster ke hisaab se chalega, aur admin ka decision final hoga. Rules todne par ban ya disqualification ho sakta hai. Paid matches sirf 18+ players ke liye hain. Match ki date, time ya format badalna pade to players ko Telegram pe pehle bataya jayega.

Refund policy

Entry fee sirf tab wapas hogi jab match hamari taraf se cancel ho, ya payment hone ke baad bhi technical wajah se slot confirm na ho paye. Refund original payment method pe 7 working days ke andar aayega. Late join, no-show, ban ya disqualification pe refund nahi milega.

Privacy policy

Hum registration ke liye team ka naam, in-game naam aur UID, Telegram username aur payment ki confirmation lete hain. Ye sirf slot confirm karne, match chalane aur payout dene ke liye use hota hai, aur hum ise kisi ko bechte nahi. Card ya UPI ki details hamare paas save nahi hoti, wo payment gateway sambhalta hai. Apna data hatwane ke liye admin ko Telegram pe message karo.
